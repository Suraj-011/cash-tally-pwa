About Indian Cash Calculator Pro

Indian Cash Calculator Pro is a simple and fast tool made for bank cashiers, shopkeepers, business owners, and anyone in India who needs to count cash easily without making mistakes.

How Cash Tally Started

"Honestly, I never planned to build this web app."

I used to visit a bank almost every day to deposit cash. During those regular visits, I became friends with Rupjyoti Rabha, a senior staff member and cashier at the bank. Since we met frequently, we started talking and gradually became good friends.

One day, during a normal conversation, he told me about a small problem.

Knowing that I am interested in web development and building useful tools, he asked me if I could create something simple that would make counting and calculating cash easier.

I told him, “Okay, I’ll try.”

That was how the idea started.

At first, I was building this tool mainly for him. I started thinking about how to make cash counting simple—just enter the number of notes, and let the tool calculate everything automatically. While I was developing it, Rupjyoti also gave me some useful suggestions about features that could make it more practical.

Slowly, that small idea turned into Cash Tally.

What started as a simple request from a friend is now a tool that I hope can help anyone who regularly deals with cash.

Sometimes, a project doesn't start with a big idea.
Sometimes, it just starts with someone saying, “Can you make something that makes my work a little easier?”

Key features built into the app:

  • Indian Denominations: Instant calculation for ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10, ₹5, ₹2, ₹1, and Loose Coins.
  • Packet & Bundle Counting: Count cash in single notes, 100-note packets (Pudda), or 1,000-note bundles (Bricks).
  • Finacle / BaNCS Bank Scroll: 1-Click copy formatted cash scroll text for Indian bank tellers.
  • Export Options: Generate high-resolution PDF reports, Excel spreadsheets, or share summaries on WhatsApp & Email.
  • Daily Register & Vault Balancing: Save daily tallies locally and track cash shortages or excesses.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and financial data confidentiality are our top priorities.

  • 100% Local Storage: All cash calculations, custom names, expected amounts, and register entries are stored strictly inside your web browser's local storage (localStorage).
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: No cash numbers, personal details, or tally data are ever transmitted or uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.
  • Offline Capable PWA: This application works completely offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can safely use it without an active internet connection.
